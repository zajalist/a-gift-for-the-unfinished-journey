# A Gift for the Unfinished Journey: Tripothon S1 submission text (spoiler-free)

**Tagline:** Carry their voices to the last transmitter.

You are Carrier 07, a walking machine in an emptied highland valley, towing a sealed archive up a 5 km mountain road to Âlaawb, the last transmitter.

Along the way the archive plays back the valley's recorded voices at the places they remember: old tapes, a community radio station, songs, names that the new signs replaced with numbers. Some of it is spoken in Saskartan, a language made for the game. Nobody else is on the road. Only wind, a Geiger counter, and the voices.

Made in Unreal Engine 5.8 on Gaea terrain, with Tripo-generated props and the author's own voice recordings. Editor automation through Hayba.
