# Car formation during startup Loading

## Current point atlas (2026-10-02)

The loading screen now uses `SourceArt/UI/T_LoadingCarrier_PointAtlas.png`, generated by
`render_loading_points.py` from the full carrier FBX. The existing Unreal texture asset keeps its
`T_LoadingCarrier_GaussianAtlas` name to preserve material references, but its pixels are discrete
surface points rather than Gaussian ellipses. The 64-frame loop adds wheel motion, body bob, camera
orbit, and nearby milestone, mirror and terrace forms. Rebuild with the script and reimport the
canonical SourceArt PNG before changing the game asset. Design-time widget preview does not render
the animated material; judge the final scale and motion in the live loading screen. The PC now holds
Loading for at least eight visible seconds, enough for two complete four-second loops.

The Gaussian instructions below document the superseded prototype.

The immediate loading visualization samples the game's **actual carrier mesh
surface** into positive anisotropic 3D Gaussian covariances, projects those
covariances into perspective ellipses, sorts by camera depth, and composites
the exponential Gaussian alpha with front-to-back transmittance. The surface
centers drift into a restrained spiral and reform into the same actual car.
There is no hand-drawn or invented car silhouette.

This loading asset is **mesh-derived, trained:false**. Its covariance, opacity
and palette are authored, not trained from multiview observations. It must not
be presented as the actual trained 3DGS memories required elsewhere in the
game. The car atlas is a fixed-view, offline pre-render of those covariances;
it is not an interactive world Gaussian renderer.

## Clarity refinement after root's actual v1 render

Root rendered the original atlas from the actual 23024-vertex/41769-triangle
carrier, transported as 1200 sampled source triangles. Source review and the
actual contact sheet showed oversized overlapping ellipse footprints, cyan
body color, and about 2.8 cm independent drift even when the car was formed.
The current renderer uses 12000 ellipsoids, smaller 0.38 tangent sigma scaling,
and a maximum projected principal sigma of 1.25 pixels (3.75-pixel support).
Scaling the complete projected covariance preserves ellipse anisotropy and
orientation. Gaussian falloff/transmittance and soft edges remain intact.

The formed silhouette now uses a coherent surface-position ripple with maximum
displacement below 4 mm at this actual carrier's size, plus 2.5% breathing.
The view defaults to azimuth 30 / elevation 12 degrees, exposing more of the
actual long UE-Y chassis in three-quarter view. The palette is warm ivory and
bronze, with readable charcoal wheels. Capsule lining and padding surfaces are
omitted from the visualization to keep their alpha from muddying the exterior;
the approved original source export is preserved and this choice is recorded
in each new render receipt. Existing atlas geometry, frame count, timings and
UI shader inputs stay compatible. Root must render and inspect atlas_v2; this
refinement was prepared without running the CPU renderer or claiming its result.
