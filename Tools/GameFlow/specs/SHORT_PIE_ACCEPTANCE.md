# Short PIE acceptance: menus, loading, radio, Human pause, end card

Run after the current native build and the scoped Caption end-card install. Use one PIE session from the title; `~` opens the UE console. The commands below already exist in SaskartaCore or CM_Saskarta. Read the resulting lines in `Saved/Logs/saskartarad.log`. Do not run automation tests or rebuild Blueprints while PIE is active.

Before PIE, in the editor console run `Automation RunTests Saskarta.Menu` and `Automation RunTests Saskarta.LoadBudget`; confirm both groups pass. `node Tools/GameFlow/verify_settings_asset.mjs` is the offline Settings readback check (current source reports 155/155).

1. **Title and Settings (about 90 s).** At the title, enter `saskarta.sensorfilter` in the console: the saved level may be above zero, but the title image must have no carrier scanlines or damaged glass (`AmountFor` excludes `Title`). Open Settings. With the keyboard, Up/Down switches tabs, Right enters rows, Down reaches the footer, and Enter activates Apply. Change Window mode, Apply, then leave **Revert** focused and wait 15 s: the old mode returns, a **Reverted** toast appears, and Settings stays open. Change it again, Apply, move focus to **Keep**, press Enter: **Applied** appears and focus returns to Apply, still in Settings. Change Shadows and press Revert; the saved value returns. With a gamepad, D-pad/left stick move one row per push, A activates, and B backs out one level. Back from Settings with a dirty row must show **Apply changes?**, where Cancel stays in Settings and Discard closes it.
2. **Loading and audio (about 60 s plus load time).** Start the game. Expect `LogSaskartaLoadBudget: Loading budget ON (Loading)` and later `OFF (Loading) ... CVar(s) restored`; after OFF, console `t.MaxFPS` should show the prior cap. Run `Saskarta.Events.Dump` and confirm `flow Playing`. Press R to turn the radio off, then R to turn it on. Listen for `SFX_Radio_Knob_Off_01` + `SFX_Radio_Power_Off_Collapse`, then `SFX_Radio_Knob_On_01` + `SFX_Radio_Power_On_Bloom`; the panel shows OFF then ON. Console `SaskartaBeat B02_ARCHIVE_OPENS` forces the existing `SFX_TapeClick` event; its archive line should duck the station. A `sound ... not imported yet` log is a failed audio asset check, even if captions advance.
3. **Human pause (about 30 s).** In the console run `Saskarta.Cine.AllowSkipAll 1`, then `Saskarta.Cine.Play CIN_19_HATCH`. After it starts, run `SaskartaSkip` before the Rise prompt. `Saskarta.Events.Dump` must say `flow Human`, and the Keeper must be possessed. Press Esc: pause opens. Press Esc again: it closes. Run `Saskarta.Events.Dump` again; `flow Human` must remain, and the Keeper can move. This probes the CIN_19 skip final state and `PausedFrom` restoration without waiting for the full ending.
4. **Early end-card skip (about 10 s).** Run `Saskarta.Cine.Play CIN_25_END_CARD`. The calligraphy appears at 2 s over black while the continuous title score rises; there is no separate valley-wind cue. After 3 s, hold Space or gamepad B for 1 s. The runner should log `cinematic CIN_25_END_CARD: skipped`, execute `OpenLevel Title`, and show the title with no end-card overlay or carrier sensor effect. This skips before CreditsRoll; the old HUD and any widget timer must disappear with the old world.

For a separate full-duration check, let CIN_25 run: the three-line card replaces the title at 10 s, the 104-line roll starts at 17 s and clears at 99 s, and Title opens at 102 s. The source for those words and timings is `docs/story/story-events.json` plus `docs/story/CREDITS_SCREEN.md`.
